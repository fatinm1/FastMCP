RAG Layer

The content API filters can't reach

SAM.gov metadata tells you an RFP exists. The 200-page PDF tells you whether you can win it. The RAG layer indexes solicitation documents and past performance write-ups for semantic search.

1
search_opportunities(naics="541512")
Find open RFPs in your capability area
2
get_opportunity_detail(notice_id)
Pull attachment links from the notice
3
ingest_solicitation(notice_id)
Download PDFs, chunk, embed, and index
4
search_solicitation_content("clearance")
Semantic search across the SOW content
5
match_past_performance(rfp_text)
Rank your best-fit past performance narratives
